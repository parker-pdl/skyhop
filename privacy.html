What the game stores

Skyhop stores your best score and your sound on/off choice on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to Parker Data Link.

Accounts

Skyhop has no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details.

Advertising

The Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob. The website shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use your device's advertising ID and general location (from your IP address) to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

Skyhop is a general-audience game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
