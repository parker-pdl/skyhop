**Full description:**
Skyhop is a one-touch sky hopping game.

Tap the screen and your hopper flaps up. Let go and it drops. Fly through the gaps between the sky towers and keep going as long as you can. Every gap you clear is one point. Touch a tower or the ground and the round is over. Tap again to try right away.

Rounds are short. Most last ten to thirty seconds. The towers speed up and the gaps get tighter the further you go, so the score you beat is your own.

Features:
• One-touch play. Tap to hop. That is the whole control scheme.
• Works with one hand, in portrait.
• Best score is saved on your device.
• Sound on or off with one tap.
• Small download. No account. No sign-in.
• Titles to earn: Tower Hopper, Cloud Captain, Sky Legend.
